# ICP — small teams: under 50 employees, knowledge work

> Status: **live profile**. This file *is* the artifact. Edit it; the pipeline re-compiles.

A detailed, explanatory definition. Not a vague persona.

## Who we sell to

Small knowledge-work companies — software, data, and vertical SaaS businesses with **fewer
than 50 employees**. Everyone there is white-collar; there is no ops layer, no RevOps hire,
and usually no marketing hire either. The company is 5–49 people who all do the work.

This is the segment most lists fail at. Directory "company size" filters bottom out in
bands that either exclude it or swallow it into `1–200`, and the buying committee is one
person. The motion has to change accordingly.

## The key difference from mid-market

At 51–200 staff the buyer is a **function** — search marketing, search growth.
At under 50 there is usually no such function, so the buyer is the **founder or CEO**, or
the first commercial hire. Search the *role that owns revenue*, and expect it to be one
person.

The other difference: **the signal matters more than the fit.** A 12-person company that
just raised a seed is a different company next quarter. Fit moves slowly; timing moves fast.

## Filters (a provider can filter on these)

| Field | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| country | `US` | Data coverage and hiring law shape |
| headcount | `1–49` | The segment. Exclude the 50–200 band entirely. |
| category | `software / data / vertical SaaS` | Knowledge work, not services |
| domain | resolves, primary | A row with no domain is not a row |
| status | currently operating | Dead domains are the most common failure in this band |

## Checks (judgement per row — run **before** any paid step)

1. **Is there a product, not a consultancy?** A pricing page, docs, or a signup. A
   "we build custom software" site is out.
2. **Is anyone commercial on staff?** A founder who sells, or one marketing/growth hire.
   If nobody owns the funnel, there is no motion to instrument yet.
3. **Is the company alive in the last 12 months?** A shipping changelog, a dated news item,
   a job post. Small companies go quiet before they die.

## Exclusions (explicit)

- Agencies, consultancies, dev shops, studios, staffing.
- Companies already in the CRM.
- Anything with no dated activity in 12 months — dormant, whatever the fit score says.
- **Raising or just raised is not a fit signal on its own.** It is a *timing* signal. It
  moves a tier; it does not qualify a row.

## The threshold

The fit threshold is a **number in the rules file**, not a vibe. This segment is noisy —
expect a lower pass rate than mid-market, and expect the tiering to do more of the work.
